## Click path (do this once)

1. Open https://www.printful.com → log in (or create account).
2. **Caps** → Product Catalog → Embroidered Dad Hat:
   - Natural: upload `pardes-guitarist-figure_hires.png` — front max **4″ × 1.75″**, figure **inside** panel, legs toward brim.
   - Jungle green: upload `tiger_hires.png` same placement.
   - Natural (optional 3rd): upload `doves_char.png`.
   - Optional: enable **3D puff** on bold shapes.
3. Accept digitizing (~$6.50/design or free at 25+). When stitch proof arrives, compare to approved mockups (blue hollow-body guitar, bare feet in water, tiger with 4 legs).
4. **Prints** → upload `pardes-jungle-3000.jpg`, `pardes-original-hires.jpg`, boat scene → poster / tote / tee mockups (free).
5. **Order 1 sample** of each cap you want (~$25–30 shipped) — **this is the only payment**.
6. After samples pass: connect Printful store (or Shopify) and swap the Shop pre-order buttons on yoavshlomov.com for real product links.
