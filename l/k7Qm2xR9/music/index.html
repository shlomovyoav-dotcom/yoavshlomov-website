NY-trained composer, guitarist and producer (BFA, The New School), based in Tel Aviv. Services

Original scoring
Music written to picture — film, animation, trailer or brand. Themes, cues and full arrangements that serve the edit.
Library & sync
Tracks written and delivered to catalog spec — full mixes, stems and alternates — as for the Artlist Original buyout.
Mix & master
Release-ready mixes and masters for songs and cues. Vocal cleanup, stems delivery, versions to spec.
Delivery
Versioned, tempo-tagged masters and alternates · stems · dialogue- and SFX-friendly mix versions · organised session hand-off · remote review.
