About

Composer, guitarist, producer.

Yoav Shlomov is a Tel Aviv-based composer and jazz guitarist. He writes and produces original music for film, animation, art and commercial work, and has 15 releases as leader — among them the EP “3/4”, bought out for the Artlist Original catalog. His single “Geshem” (with Adi Renert) aired on Kan 88FM.

He studied at The New School (BFA, New York, merit scholarship) and works across Ableton Live, Pro Tools, Logic, Luna and Sibelius, with the Waves mixing ecosystem he supported professionally at Waves Audio — delivering everything from a single cue to versioned masters, stems and dialogue-friendly mixes.
