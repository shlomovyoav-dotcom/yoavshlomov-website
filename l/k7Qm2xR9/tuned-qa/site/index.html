The method

How I get a product in tune

Four stages, in order. Same way you tune an instrument: set the reference, build the thing, hold it there, then teach the ear so it stays tuned without me.

E

Set the pitch

Audit & risk strategy

I map where quality actually breaks in your product — for me that's usually the money paths: plans, credits, checkout, subscription logic. We define the risk model and decide, honestly, what's worth automating and what isn't.

A

Build the kit

AI-augmented automation

Playwright end-to-end suites plus AI agents that turn specs and tickets into real, maintainable tests — the same kind of toolkit I ran inside a high-traffic billing and growth product, not a slide about one.

D

Hold the tuning

CI quality gates

The checks get wired into your pipeline so regressions are caught before release — at the gate, not by your users on a Friday afternoon.

G

Teach the ear

Team enablement

I leave your engineers able to run and extend the AI-assisted workflow themselves. The goal isn't to make you depend on me — it's that quality doesn't walk out the door when I do.

Money paths

Pricing & checkout audit

I map where plans, prices, credits, checkout and subscription changes can break, test the riskiest paths by hand, and hand over a prioritised test plan in Gherkin, ready for Zephyr, Xray or a spreadsheet.

AI quality

AI agent check

Your support bot or in-product assistant, run against your real tickets and prompts: a labeled set of the answers it gets wrong, the edge cases that break it (billing, identity, refunds), and bug reports a vendor can act on.
